# Eurostar Snap Tracker - Style Guide

## Premium Brand Philosophy
The design must feel fast, premium, and reliable—evoking the speed and savings of finding high-speed train tickets.

### 1. Color Palette

**Primary Colors:**
- **Eurostar Deep Blue (`#001A70`)**: Use for headers, primary buttons, and strong emphasis.
- **Vibrant Accent Yellow (`#FFD700`)**: Use for alerts, highlights, and secondary calls-to-action to grab attention.

**Secondary & Background Colors:**
- **App Background (`#F4F6F9`)**: A slightly cool, premium light gray to let content cards stand out.
- **Card/Element Background (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure white for tracking result cards and email bodies.
- **Subtle Borders (`#E5E7EB`)**: For delineating sections without looking harsh.

**Typography Colors:**
- **Text Primary (`#1F2937`)**: Dark slate gray for extreme readability (avoiding pure black).
- **Text Secondary (`#6B7280`)**: Muted gray for timestamps, metadata, and fine print.

### 2. Typography
- **Primary Font**: `Inter` or `Outfit` (via Google Fonts). 
*(Fallback for email clients: `-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`)*
- Weight usage: Bold for prices and times, Regular for standard text.

### 3. Email Template Specs
Every email output natively wraps its raw messaging into a branded container:
- Centered white card over the `#F4F6F9` background.
- Rounded corners (`8px`) with soft shadow (`rgba(0,0,0,0.05)`).
- A striking `#001A70` header.
- Stripe links automatically transformed into large `#001A70` buttons with bold styling.
